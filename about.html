Our Story

From a single crew to a trusted Orlando cleaning partner.

Luxury Duo Cleaning LLC was started with a simple belief: that cleaning is a service business, and service means showing up, doing the work right, and earning the next job.

Based in Orlando, we started serving residential clients and quickly expanded into commercial and construction cleaning as contractors recognized the quality of our work. Today, we serve general contractors, property managers, Airbnb hosts, and homeowners across the greater Orlando area.

We're still growing, and we're still hands-on. Every job gets the same attention to detail whether it's a single-family home or a 10,000-square-foot commercial buildout.
